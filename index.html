Misha Lantsov

Movement teacher, bodyworker, and educator.

I work with people through hands-on bodywork, movement practice, and physical education, with the aim of helping people become more capable of sensing, understanding, and working with their own bodies.

I have worked hands-on in massage and bodywork for seven years, am myofascial-release certified, train and teach at Ape Co, and am a fourth-year student in Marcello Palozzo’s five-year Human Movement Studies program. I founded and teach Denver Movement School.
