Misha Lantsov

I’m a movement teacher, bodyworker, and educator in Colorado. I help people reconnect with their bodies through touch, movement practice, and creative attention.

I run Misha Bodywork and Movement, a therapeutic bodywork practice at 1300 Jackson St, Suite B200 in downtown Golden, and Denver Movement School, where I teach movement practice for adults in Denver.

You can learn more about me, book bodywork in Golden, explore movement classes, or read my writing.

I also share video content and photos from my creative and movement work.
