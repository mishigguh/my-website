Who it’s for

You want to do more
with your movement.

You’re interested in parkour, acrobatics, strength, or moving more creatively—and want guidance on what to practice next.

You’re willing to practice independently, film your work, and use feedback to improve. We build from your starting point, goals, and available environment.

How coaching works

A clear plan. Practice. Feedback.

  1. 01

    Find your starting point

    An interview and movement assessment help us understand your goals, current abilities, and where you train.

  2. 02

    Get your next phase

    Individualized modules give you drills, demonstration videos, and milestones. You know what to work on and what progress looks like.

  3. 03

    Practice, film, refine

    Send footage of your practice. We review it, give specific feedback, and adjust the work as you progress.

What we teach

Skills that connect.

Environmental parkour · Jumping & landing · Acrobatics & inversion · Strength · Rolling · Climbing · Creative movement

Your modules draw from these subjects according to your goals and assessment. The aim: bring skills together into adaptable, expressive movement.

The commitment

Give your practice time to develop.

Monthly coaching with a minimum initial commitment of three months, followed by ongoing coaching at a monthly rate.

  • Interview & movement assessment
  • Individualized modules & demonstrations
  • Milestones to guide your progress
  • Video review & ongoing adjustments
