Writing

Writing is one of the ways I work through questions about movement, pain, perception, learning, bodywork, and what it means to develop a practice.

  • Rethinking Posture — posture as a dynamic behavior rather than a fixed ideal.
  • Beyond Exercise — movement as practice, learning, play, and a broader human capacity.
  • Inducing Somatic Release — exploratory notes on somatic experience and hands-on work.

For the practical side of this work, see Misha Bodywork and Movement or Denver Movement School.
