About

Misha Lantsov

I’m a movement teacher, bodyworker, and educator in Colorado. My work is organized around a simple question: how can people become more capable of sensing, understanding, and working with their own bodies?

I run Misha Bodywork and Movement in Golden, where I offer therapeutic bodywork for pain, tension, injuries, and recurring patterns. I also founded Denver Movement School, where adults practice strength, coordination, locomotion, partner work, athleticism, and movement as an ongoing field of study.

Background

Practice, study, and teaching.

I have seven years of hands-on massage and bodywork experience and am certified in myofascial release. I have led workshops on couples bodywork and self-treatment using therapy balls and other tools.

I’m a fourth-year student in the five-year Human Movement Studies program with Marcello Palozzo. I also train and teach at Ape Co, and I design curriculum and teach through Denver Movement School.

My own training has crossed bodywork, strength, mobility, acrobatics, dance, endurance, athletics, and somatic practice. I treat my education as active and unfinished rather than something completed once and displayed as authority.

I’m interested in movement not only as exercise, but as a way to develop attention, adaptability, agency, creativity, and a more direct relationship with the body.

Now

What I’m working on.

Updated August 2026

Right now I’m focused on continuing my own movement studies, teaching Denver Movement School, developing a more integrated bodywork practice in Golden, and writing about pain, perception, movement, and learning.
