About

Bodywork and movement education are two ways into the same problem.

I help people understand what changes when they receive hands-on work, pay closer attention, and begin moving with more options.

My work

I’ve spent seven years working hands-on with people through massage and bodywork. I’m myofascial-release certified, teach movement, and founded Denver Movement School.

My bodywork may draw from therapeutic massage, myofascial work, sports-massage techniques, assisted mobility, self-myofascial release, breath, relaxation, and movement education. I don’t treat those as separate menu items; they are tools chosen around the person in front of me.

Current study

I currently study Human Movement Studies with Marcello Palozzo and train within the Ape Co movement community. That study informs how I think about strength, coordination, adaptability, attention, physical preparation, and learning.

For clients, that means bodywork and movement do not have to stay separate. We can use both to understand what helps you feel and move better, then give the useful changes somewhere to go.
