How It Works

Follow the pattern instead of starting over.

The order may change based on what we learn.

  • 01 · Understand & begin. Review your history, work hands-on, and reassess in a 75-minute session.
  • 02 · Follow the pattern. Work from what changed and what returned, often adding a simple therapy-ball practice for between visits.
  • 03 · Build on what changed. Continue from what we’ve learned instead of starting over.
  • 04 · Put it into movement. Apply what we’ve learned in a 60-minute individualized movement session—closer to personal training than a group class.
Continuity

Enough time to notice what holds.

The Intensive gives us enough continuity to see what changes and returns, work hands-on more than once, add self-release, and apply what we learn to movement.

Each visit begins with the information from the last one, so we can keep following the pattern instead of treating every session as a fresh start.

Fit & First-Session Policy

A structured option, without getting locked into the wrong fit.

The Intensive may be useful if...

  • You want continuity rather than a series of disconnected appointments.
  • You want to understand what changes, what returns, and what to practice between visits.
  • You want hands-on work to connect with individualized movement.

If it is not the right fit...

You’ll pay $560 up front. If either of us decides after the first visit that the Intensive isn’t the right fit, I’ll refund $415; you’ll pay only the usual $145 for that session.

Not ready for four sessions? You can begin with one 75-minute individual appointment.

FAQ

A few common questions.

Do I have to know whether four sessions will be a fit?

No. The first-session policy keeps you from being locked into the wrong fit: if either of us decides after the first visit not to continue, I refund $415 and the first visit remains charged at the usual $145.

Can I start with one individual session instead?

Yes. The Intensive is an optional structured next step. You can book a first individual session without committing to four visits.

Is the movement session a group class?

No. It is a 60-minute individualized movement-practice session based on what we learned together—closer to personal training than a group class.

What does the between-visit practice involve?

It may include in-person therapy-ball self-release instruction and a simple practice based on what we notice in your bodywork sessions.
