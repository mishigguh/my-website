One-Month Movement & Bodywork Intensive

A concentrated month for people who want more support than a single appointment or weekly class can provide.

The intensive brings together individualized movement practice, therapeutic bodywork, access to group practice, and a personal plan. Instead of treating pain, tension, fitness, stress, and movement confidence as unrelated problems, we use the month to understand how they interact and build a practice around what matters most to you.

Who it may be useful for

This format is most useful when you are working with several things at once: recurring pain or tension, an injury or surgery history, high stress, feeling disconnected from your body, rebuilding physical capacity, or wanting to return to activities that have become difficult or uncertain.

It can also be a fit if you have already seen multiple practitioners or tried several approaches and want a more integrated process that combines hands-on work with learning what to do between sessions.

How the month works

The exact structure is built around the person rather than a fixed protocol. A month typically includes regular one-on-one movement and bodywork sessions, relevant Denver Movement School classes, a simple personal practice plan, and ongoing adjustments based on what we learn.

The aim is not to make you dependent on more appointments. It is to use a concentrated period of support to create clarity, build useful habits and physical capacity, and leave you with a more sustainable way to keep working.

What we may work on

  • reducing recurring pain, tension, or physical restriction when bodywork and movement are appropriate
  • returning gradually to walking, running, training, sport, or other meaningful activity
  • strength, mobility, coordination, breathing, relaxation, and physical preparedness
  • learning to notice patterns of bracing, effort, avoidance, or overuse earlier
  • building a realistic practice that fits your actual life rather than an idealized program
