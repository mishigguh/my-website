One-Month Movement & Bodywork Intensive

A concentrated month for people who want more support than a single appointment or weekly class can provide.

Start an Inquiry
The format

Several pieces working together.

The intensive brings together individualized movement practice, therapeutic bodywork, access to group practice, and a personal plan. Instead of treating pain, tension, fitness, stress, and movement confidence as unrelated problems, we use the month to understand how they interact and build a practice around what matters most to you.

Who it may be useful for

A concentrated period when several things are interacting.

Recurring patterns

Pain, tension, stress, physical restriction, or feeling disconnected from your body.

Returning to activity

Rebuilding confidence and capacity for walking, running, training, sport, or other meaningful activity.

Too many separate solutions

You have tried multiple practitioners or approaches and want a process that connects hands-on work with what you do between sessions.

Practice that fits real life

You want a realistic personal practice that can survive work, family, travel, stress, and changing energy.

How the month works

Built around the person, not a fixed protocol.

A month typically includes regular one-on-one movement and bodywork sessions, relevant Denver Movement School classes, a simple personal practice plan, and ongoing adjustments based on what we learn.

The aim is not to make you dependent on more appointments. It is to use a concentrated period of support to create clarity, build useful habits and physical capacity, and leave you with a more sustainable way to keep working.

By inquiry

Start with a short conversation.

Capacity is intentionally limited. The first step is a short inquiry so I can understand what you are working with and whether a concentrated month makes sense.
