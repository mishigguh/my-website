Environmental Movement Practice brings together strength, technical skill, and creative exploration. We work with you remotely, building individualized phases of practice around your starting point, your goals, and the environments available to you.

It’s for people drawn to parkour, acrobatics, strength, or moving more creatively—who want guidance on what to practice next, and are willing to practice independently, film their work, and learn through feedback.

Working with the possibilities of the environment.

The practice grows through a conversation.

We begin with an interview and movement assessment: a chance to understand what you’re working toward, how you move, and where you train.

From there, we build your next phase. Individualized modules, written practice, and demonstration videos give you something clear to work with. Milestones help you recognize what’s changing.

You practice in your own time and send us footage. We review it, offer specific feedback, and refine the work with you. Each phase becomes the starting point for the next.

Environmental parkour, jumping & landing,
acrobatics & inversion, strength,
rolling, climbing, creative movement.

Your modules draw from these subjects according to your goals and assessment, bringing skills together into adaptable, expressive movement.

Give your practice time to develop.

Coaching is offered at a monthly rate, with a minimum initial commitment of three months, followed by ongoing monthly coaching.

This includes your interview and assessment, individualized modules and demonstrations, milestones, video review, and ongoing adjustments.
